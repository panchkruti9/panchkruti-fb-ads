Use [Ai Meta Ads](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1kRcRPFpW960m7xtZ6EXHm_MglaYuIdhR) and its subfolders as the sole Google Drive source for Meta ad creative. When a new image is needed, base its concept and design on the team's creative from this folder and keep the product and packaging accurate.

Create only static display ads, video ads, or Story ads. Catalogue ads are excluded.

## Naming

Use one lowercase, hyphenated campaign ID beginning with `agi-` for new work. Use it as the Meta campaign name and as the base for the corresponding plan, evidence, notes, and asset paths. Keep existing IDs where reports already depend on them.

Use `<campaign-id>-<audience>` for ad sets and `<campaign-id>-<stage>-<concept>-<format>-v01` for ads. Use the ad name as the base for its brief and media. Increase the version for revisions. Keep each original Drive filename and record its link in the brief.

For example, use `agi-duo-acq` for the campaign and `agi-duo-acq-consideration-routine-story-v01` for the Story and its brief and export.

Add any must-follow rules you already have, such as a market to exclude. The agent researches the offer, audience, market, and past results. It proposes the campaign goal, daily budget, tests, success target, and when to increase spending.
