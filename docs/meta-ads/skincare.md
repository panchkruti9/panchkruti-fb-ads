# Panchkruti skincare ads

## Plan the campaign

Use the [strategy playbook](strategy.md) to choose the campaign goal, targeting, budget, and first test. Check the approved formula and packaging separately for each item in a bundle before choosing what the ad can demonstrate.

## Check each creative and the destination

| Evidence field | Record what was verified |
|---|---|
| `product_accuracy` | Use actual pack photography and current approved product facts. Check each component’s label, size, ingredient attribution, and instructions. Cross-check the Shopify offer, price, availability, mobile checkout, and visible landing-page copy. |
| `claims` | Link evidence for the exact claims in copy, audio, captions, visuals, and the destination. An ingredient reference alone cannot substantiate a finished product result. Keep language about the appearance of dark spots and use a claim such as “helps improve the look of dark spots” only with support. Escalate treatment claims, guarantees, and timed or quantified results for verification. |
| `imagery` | Check the actual placement preview. Panchkruti uses real skin texture and avoids before-and-after transformations, altered results, skin-shaming, and suggestions that lighter skin is superior. Avoid language that implies the viewer has a skin condition or flaw. |
| `rights_and_disclosures` | Record the source and usage rights for each asset. For reviews or creator content, confirm authentic wording, permission, and any required material-connection disclosure. |

Test distinct purchase reasons, such as a two-step routine demonstration, an accurately sourced formula explanation, or an authorized customer experience. Verify each concept’s proof before testing. Record unresolved checks as open and send exact previews to the authorized reviewer. [Meta Advertising Standards training](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/248744-meta-advertising-standards-course) · [ASCI Code and guidelines](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code-guidelines/) · [CDSCO cosmetics guidance](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Cosmetics/cosmetics/)

For the Duo, verify the serum and cream separately against their current pack and formula records. Use the storefront pages to check product messaging and verify the actual Duo destination before activating ads. ## Verify purchase tracking and reporting

Use the Shopify Meta channel’s authorized data-sharing setup. In Meta Events Manager, validate Purchase, currency, and value using a test order. Check browser/server deduplication when both sources send Purchase. Confirm that the ad’s campaign identifier survives the landing page and checkout. Use a Shopify campaign-filtered order export or equivalent aggregate report. Define paid first orders, cancellations, refunds, and cash-on-delivery maturity where applicable before choosing the review lag. Compare Shopify results and Meta-attributed purchases side by side under their respective attribution settings. Use store-wide results for business context. [Shopify Meta data sharing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/analyze-marketing/meta-data-sharing) · [Shopify marketing reports](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/marketing-reports)
