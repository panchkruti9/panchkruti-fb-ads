# Meta Ads creative brief

Save one copy per ad version with the [standard ad name](brand-rules.md#naming).

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Campaign and ad name | Campaign ID, planned ad name, and link to the campaign plan |
| Buyer and message | Funnel stage, purchase reason, hook, and supporting evidence |
| Source | Exact Drive link, original filename, and local copy |
| What to preserve | Product and visual details, sequence, and sound where relevant |
| What to change | Reuse or adapt, intended placement and format, and the variable under test |
| Final copy | Primary text, headline or on-screen copy as applicable, CTA, and destination |
| Review | Product and claims checks, usage rights, placement preview, and any open issue |
| Deliverables | Final media and preview filenames or links, with the status of each |
