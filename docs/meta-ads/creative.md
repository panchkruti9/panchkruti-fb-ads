# Meta Ads creative workflow

Start with the concept and test in the campaign plan. Use one [creative brief](creative-template.md) per ad version. Use the [brand rules](brand-rules.md) for format, Drive scope, and naming.

1. Find a suitable original in the approved Drive folder. Use `01 Discovery`, `02 Consideration`, or `03 Purchase` to match the job of the ad. Treat files in `04 Review before use` as pending. Record the exact Drive link and filename. If a suitable source is missing or inaccessible, leave the brief open and identify the asset needed.
2. Download the original at its available quality. Keep its filename in a local working folder under `assets/meta-ads/<campaign-id>/source/`. Inspect the actual image or footage. Record the product, visual direction, sequence, and audio or copy to carry forward as applicable.
3. Reuse the original if it fits the brief and placement. Otherwise, adapt it for the planned static, video, or Story placement. Record what changes and why, including the variable under test. Use the downloaded Panchkruti asset to guide visual production. Imported guides can help frame the test.
4. Inspect the exported file. Check its readability and crop in the intended placement, along with product accuracy, claims, rights, and destination. Use the [skincare checks](skincare.md#check-each-creative-and-the-destination). Mark unresolved checks in the brief.
5. Save the brief under `campaigns/<campaign-id>/creative/` and the final media and preview under `assets/meta-ads/<campaign-id>/exports/` and `previews/`. Deliver the downloadable media, final copy, and placement preview together. Use the [runbook](runbook.md) for any authorized Ads Manager handoff.
