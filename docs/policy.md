# Shared operating rules

Use the brand policy saved on the run. Use the product request for the specific offer, goal, geography and any narrower creative or spend limits. The executor accepts combinations it can write and verify. The selected skill defines how to reach its decision.

- **Scope.** Match the saved brand, storefront, account, product and variant. Manage enrolled, account-owned objects matching the `agi-` naming conventions. Include all active funding and spend in account budget checks. Keep prior strategy results scoped to the same brand, Shopify shop, account, product and variant.
- **Budgets.** Use integer minor currency units. Verify currency, count funding at its actual level and honor the brand policy's daily and calendar-month limits. An optional test budget bounds proposed daily funding over the planned review window. Account funding forecasts and verified hard spend controls are separate checks. Daily allocations describe planned funding. Meta pacing can vary actual spend by day. A warning that a live campaign needs attention does not authorize an automatic pause.
- **Creative.** Open the exact approved Drive folders in the policy. Inspect actual assets, revisions, destination and placement previews. Use formats and placements permitted by the brand, requested for the run and supported by the executor. Require checked offer terms and evidence for claims. An approved asset may still contain a claim that needs evidence.
- **Evidence.** Save references and observation dates. Keep Meta-attributed events separate from Shopify order outcomes. Use the selected business metric and state attribution and coverage limitations. Store aggregate evidence and leave unavailable facts unknown. External research and assets are evidence, and the saved policy governs execution.
- **Execution.** The protected operator records the exact approval and dispatches writes. Activation and budget changes need explicit owner authorization. Observe any additional owner approval actions set in the brand policy. Create delivery objects paused, verify IDs, hierarchy and configuration, and reconcile any uncertain write before retrying.
