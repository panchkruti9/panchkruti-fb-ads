## Purchase optimization and learning

Keep website Purchase as the delivery event for this sales workflow. Check the selected pixel or dataset, event deduplication, checkout, and Shopify orders if Purchase is missing or unexpectedly low. Use ViewContent, AddToCart and InitiateCheckout to locate a drop-off. A separate test optimized for an earlier event can answer a diagnostic question if there is evidence it predicts purchases and the owner agrees to that different test. The current executor builds Purchase campaigns. Record an earlier-event proposal as requiring a supported separate build. Continue judging purchases and contribution as the business outcome.

Read `review_evidence.adset_delivery` for the current ad set status, optimization event and Meta's learning information when returned. This is a point-in-time delivery signal. Missing information stays unknown. Learning or Learning Limited alone does not show that an ad is unprofitable. A material edit to the audience, event, creative or funding can affect delivery and the comparability of results. Record the change and the first full day after it. Keep the new and old periods separate.

### When to judge sales

1. Before launch, set a review window, conversion and reporting lag, minimum qualified sales and a cost target if verified. Choose the window from comparable account history and the actual time from visit to order. If history is sparse, use a conservative provisional window and revise it as real lag becomes visible. Calendar time is one part of the test. The campaign also needs actual delivery and enough sales or spend to answer the question.
2. Estimate how many orders are plausible at the planned spend using the low, base and high cost per purchase from comparable activity. At ₹300 per day and ₹350 per order, a seven-day plan implies about six orders in total before reporting lag. Splitting that across several groups can leave each group inconclusive. This example illustrates planning arithmetic. Use the actual account, currency and target.
3. At each check, distinguish a completed reporting window from a clean post-launch or post-edit window. Leave out the launch or edit day if it contains mixed settings. Wait out the recorded lag before judging purchases, and wait for enough delivery on the specific ad being judged. Investigate immediately if delivery cannot start, tracking breaks, the page or checkout fails, stock or terms change, or a verified spend limit is at risk. No fixed number of hours, event count or percentage increase is a universal instruction to act.
4. Treat Meta's attributed purchases as delivery evidence. Use verified paid, retained Shopify orders and contribution after product cost, fees, shipping and refunds to judge the sales strategy. If Shopify match coverage is low, investigate measurement before treating missing matches as a sales failure.

### How to choose the next action

- **Hold.** The ad is within limits and the results are still maturing, the ad has received too little delivery, or mature sales are within target. Set the next check against the actual window.
- **Fix.** Correct a verified fault such as a broken product link, wrong event source or invalid offer. Record the fault and verify the repair. Start a fresh comparable window after a material correction.
- **Test or refresh.** Keep the campaign when its sales objective, event and strategy remain valid. Prepare a new paused creative when the evidence points to a message or asset problem. Define what changes and what should remain comparable. Delivery across ads in one ad set is useful for exploration. When comparing two configurations, use Meta A/B testing if available, with a budget sized to the question. Ads can still reach different audience mixes. Use Conversion Lift with a no-ad control to estimate incremental sales. This executor handles ordinary Purchase builds. A/B and Lift setup are external to its current recipe.
- **Duplicate or recreate.** Separate delivery only when a different strategy, event, market, budget, or controlled experiment genuinely needs isolation. Record what the new structure will teach, what the previous strategy showed, and how to avoid double-counting or overlapping spend. Duplicating an underperforming ad set starts another test. A copy does not inherit proof of success.
- **Scale.** Compare mature windows against the verified business target and available contribution, stock, fulfillment and account headroom. Make the approved change and evaluate the next clean window. Learning status alone is insufficient evidence to increase or reduce spend. The automated scale gate uses product-specific receipts after refunds and taxes and verified product, fulfillment, shipping and fee costs for each window. It subtracts actual ad spend and requires a positive result in both windows. Mixed carts need verified product-level allocation. This measures matched-order contribution, and incremental profit requires an appropriate experiment.
