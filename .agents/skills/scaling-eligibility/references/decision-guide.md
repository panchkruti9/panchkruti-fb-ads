# Decide whether more spend has a business case

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Require business proof

Read the strategy's mature comparison design, matched retained outcomes, tracking quality and verified economics. Check contribution after ad spend, not only Meta ROAS. Pending refunds, mixed-cart allocation or incomplete matching can weaken the conclusion.

## Check capacity

Confirm product stock, fulfillment, stable offer/checkout and the account's actual delivery state. Historical success does not authorize spending on unavailable stock.

## Learning status

Learning or Learning Limited is context. It does not automatically prevent a profitable campaign from being considered, and exiting learning does not prove profitability.

## Handoff

If eligible, state what is repeatable and the main scaling risk. Otherwise choose continued monitoring or a bounded diagnosis with its next date, preserving uncertainty instead of manufacturing a target.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Check qualified purchase volume, contribution after advertising, attribution coverage, repeatability, current stock/fulfillment and tracking quality. Learning status alone does not disqualify or validate a campaign. Missing costs permit bounded learning but not a claim of profitable expansion.

Default to two independent mature windows. A preselected single-window strategy needs twice the ordinary minimum qualified outcomes and a dated repeatability_reference, plus positive contribution and other checks; it is not causal proof. If only a small subset of orders matches, report the limitation and investigate measurement rather than calling all other sales failures or assigning them all to Meta.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
