# Establish the sellable offer

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Where to look

Read the assigned Shopify product and variant, live storefront, packaging and saved shipping/returns terms. Compare price, quantity, availability and destination. Distinguish a bundle from its individual components.

## How to reason

Describe what the customer receives, how it is used, the practical benefit and the evidence available. An ingredient explanation can inspire research; it does not prove a guaranteed result for the finished product. Separate confirmed facts from positioning hypotheses.

## When to proceed

Research and paused preparation can continue with explicitly unknown margin or sparse reviews. Resolve an unpurchasable offer, wrong product, contradictory price or missing shipping market before launch. Send the exact ambiguity to the agent for investigation.

## Useful handoff

Give Strategy Development the offer, differentiated facts, price friction, purchase route and unknowns. A list of technical IDs without an explanation of why someone might buy is incomplete.
