# Choose media that can carry the idea

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Inspect the original

Open the actual image, or inspect video and audio sufficiently to understand its content. Verify product identity, mobile readability, composition, placement fit and the selected concept. A filename is not visual evidence.

## Respect existing artwork

Use suitable owner-approved assets as supplied. Assess ordinary wording at its normal product-marketing level; raise a specific material misleading or policy problem rather than a blanket demand for new clinical evidence.

## Connector fallback

If Drive omits version/checksum, read the current revision before and after downloading original bytes, verify stable revision and size, and compute local hashes. If the viewer fails, use the media-preview output. Continue with another usable candidate when one fails.

## Organize new assets

Use the configured funnel folder and product subfolder. Check for a matching filename/bytes first; preserve originals and create the next revision for changed artwork. Save exact provider ID, revision and placement rationale.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Inspect original pixels or meaningful video/audio content, not a filename. Compare concept fit, product identity, embedded wording, mobile readability and placement crop. Existing suitable owner-approved artwork remains reusable; flag a concrete material problem rather than demanding new research for every ordinary benefit.

Record exact file_id/revision/stage and origin: brand or agent. Agent-generated visuals require a signed approve-creative receipt for exact original bytes/revision; reuse that receipt only while unchanged. Before filing a new version, check whether identical bytes already exist. If metadata is omitted, read revision before/after authenticated download, verify size and hash bytes. A failed candidate or viewer should lead to preview fallback or another eligible asset, with the limitation recorded.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
