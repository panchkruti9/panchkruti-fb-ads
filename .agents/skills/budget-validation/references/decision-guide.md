# Check the proposed increase against live exposure

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Read fresh funding

Use the GitHub Meta gateway to verify current account commitments, spend to date, budget ownership and currency. Cached research alone is insufficient for a spending decision.

## Avoid double counting

Campaign-budget campaigns contribute their campaign allocation; ad-set-budget campaigns contribute applicable ad-set funding. Count all active account exposure. Verify the target still has the assumed current budget before substituting the proposal.

## Compare limits

Check projected daily/monthly exposure against the saved policy and any narrower request. Separate a forecast from verified provider spend controls. An allocation is not a promise of exact daily pacing.

## Handoff

Return actual amounts, the exact proposed change and pass/fail reasons. When headroom changed, revise the proposal rather than silently reducing a different campaign or asking for a generic approval.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Read live funding at its true owner: campaign budget once, or active ad-set allocations. Include unmanaged account exposure when checking policy, and use actual calendar-month spend plus remaining funding forecast. A budget commitment is not exact daily pacing. Preserve AGI campaign spending separately from account counters.

The approved operating allocation is narrower than the policy maximum. ALLOCATE cannot increase aggregate funding and decreases run first. For higher funding show exact before/after and total impact. Native spending controls and prepaid funding differ; never post a postpay-only spend-cap update to prepaid accounts or treat a stale balance display as an independently verified delivery fault.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
