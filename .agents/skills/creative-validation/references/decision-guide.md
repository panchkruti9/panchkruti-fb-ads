# Verify the complete ad experience

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Check together

Review the exact media, embedded text, caption, headline, CTA, destination and offer as one ad. Check authentic packaging, product/variant match, readable crop, accurate price/terms and usable landing-page context.

## Distinguish ownership

For supplied approved assets, flag concrete material problems. For agent-written copy and generated visuals, check every new representation against product evidence and Meta standards. Platform review is an operational signal, not an efficacy or profit certification.

## Build compatibility

Map each ad to its exact selected asset revision, funnel stage, ad set, permitted placement and format. Reels need a supported video recipe. Do not silently replace an unsupported format with a different approved experiment.

## Release decision

Release usable ads to paused build and list limitations. Isolate an unusable creative so another valid concept can proceed. Meta preview inspection belongs before activation; a successful image upload does not establish visual quality.
