# Connect ads to retained business outcomes

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Read the same period

Use Shopify product/variant orders for the same complete Meta dates and timezone. Keep matched campaign orders, unmatched orders, other-campaign orders and pending journeys separate.

## Measure revenue honestly

Use retained paid product outcomes after refunds/taxes with product-level allocation. A mixed cart's entire order value is not revenue from the advertised product. Report incomplete allocations and buyer-history gaps.

## Contribution

Where verified costs exist, compare retained product receipts against product, fulfillment, shipping and payment costs plus actual ad spend. Avoid counting a refund twice. Gross ROAS can improve while contribution falls.

## Handoff

State the selected success metric, matching coverage, verified contribution and uncertainty. Missing campaign matches can be an attribution problem; do not describe all unmatched orders as proof the ads failed.
