# Turn the buying hypothesis into a delivery design

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Start with the decision

Read the chosen buyer situation, promise, evidence, economics and test question from Strategy Development. Decide what each campaign or ad set must isolate. Explain the learning gained from every extra budget split.

## Choose the structure

Prefer a consolidated Purchase structure when budget and signal cannot fund several useful comparisons. Separate a market, acquisition goal, warm audience or test when its message, authority or measurement needs independent delivery. TOFU/MOFU/BOFU do not require three campaigns.

## Choose budget ownership

Use campaign budget when shared allocation is intended; use ad-set funding when a justified test needs controlled allocation. Estimate plausible event volume from explicitly labeled CPA scenarios. Scenarios do not become verified financial targets.

## Reuse or replace

Continue a valid strategy in its existing campaign where possible. Recreate only for a material hypothesis or structure change, preserving the predecessor and avoiding overlapping spend. Save the intended objective, event, structure, allocation and success criteria in the contract.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Decide which variable each budget split isolates and estimate whether it can receive meaningful exposure. Choose campaign allocation when Meta should share funding; choose ad-set funding when controlled allocation answers a useful question. TOFU/MOFU/BOFU can share one Purchase campaign. A separate warm campaign needs usable audience and message/budget reasons.

Consult the returned capability inventory before selecting a recipe. Current recipes include image/video, Feed carousel, verified existing-catalogue Sales, website-event audience creation, existing lookalikes and within-campaign ad-set redistribution. A campaign-budget campaign can receive a new paused ad set without increasing its budget. Do not treat an old temporary format choice as a researched decision for a new cycle. Carry exact campaign/ad names and funding ownership into validation.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
