# Practical decisions

## Applied purchase decisions

Compare candidate buying hypotheses by evidence, relevance, available proof, purchase friction and feasibility at the approved spend. Choose the strongest, record the strongest alternative and produce decision_contract with buyer, situation, objection, offer, message/proof, delivery, question and continue/change/stop conditions. Estimate plausible purchase volume using labeled low/base/high CPA scenarios; keep scenarios outside executable verified CPA targets.

When arrivals do not become checkouts, compare message match, delivered price, trust and page clarity before blaming targeting. When checkout starts do not become paid orders, investigate payment/shipping/stock. With sparse data, define bounded learning and a future decision condition. On the next cycle, carry forward actual learning and explain any repeated previously unsuccessful hypothesis.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
