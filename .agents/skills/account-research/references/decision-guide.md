# Understand what already exists

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Where to look

Read the approved account, managed agi- hierarchy, configured/effective status, recent complete delivery windows and change history. Read account-wide funding even for unmanaged campaigns.

## Interpret the history

Map campaign to product, market, objective and material edits before comparing outcomes. A paused object with no delivery is an untested draft. A campaign with old attribution settings or a different offer is not a clean baseline.

## Existing campaigns

Inspect and enroll a matching managed campaign before deciding to keep, improve or replace it. A name match alone does not establish the product destination. Other campaigns remain outside mutation scope.

## Useful handoff

Explain what is reusable, what has already failed, what is untested and the actual funding headroom. Record any data gap without inventing a performance history.
