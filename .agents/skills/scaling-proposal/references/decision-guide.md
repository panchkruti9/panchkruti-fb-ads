# Make the spending decision reviewable

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Show the change

Name the campaign and exact budget owner, current amount, proposed amount and currency. State account-wide projected impact and the approved limit it must fit.

## Explain the case

Summarize the mature business outcomes and contribution supporting the increase, the delivery uncertainty and stock capacity. Distinguish matched contribution from incremental profit.

## Make approval concrete

Display the exact saved proposal and next review time. Ask for the required specific decision after preparation, avoiding repeated requests for authorization already tied to the same unchanged proposal.

## If declined

Keep the current authorized funding and saved strategy. Record the decision and schedule the next useful review without treating a declined increase as a broken system.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Show the exact target and funding level, current/new daily allocation, currency, effect on total exposure and remaining calendar-month authority. Summarize qualified purchases, net receipts, costs, contribution, matching coverage and the repeatability case. Show forecast uncertainty and fulfillment risks.

Prepare the saved proposal before asking. The owner approves an increased total, not a vague request to keep managing. If the increase is declined, retain existing authorized funding and continue ordinary management. Do not describe a transfer within the unchanged approved total as requiring another owner budget decision.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
