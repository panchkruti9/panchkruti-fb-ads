# Choose an action the evidence supports

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Hold

Continue within authority when the result is immature, underexposed or meeting the strategy. Set a real next review from the complete window and lag; do not ask the owner whether to perform every routine check.

## Fix or test

Act on a verified operational problem promptly. For performance changes, use a comparable complete window and diagnosis. A tracked zero-order window can justify a fresh test without a fabricated CPA ceiling; attribution uncertainty still needs investigation.

## Recreate

Use a new campaign only when the strategy or required isolation materially changes. Record the failed hypothesis, chosen alternative and disposition of the old campaign. Duplicating to reset learning is not a reasoned strategy.

## Scale or stop

Scale requires verified business contribution and authority. A proposed pause must name its evidence and exact object; prepare the saved approval route. Uncertainty alone does not authorize uncontrolled spending or an unapproved mutation.
