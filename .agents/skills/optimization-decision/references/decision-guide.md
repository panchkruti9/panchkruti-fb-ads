# Choose an action the evidence supports

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Hold

Continue within authority when the result is immature, underexposed or meeting the strategy. Set a real next review from the complete window and lag; do not ask the owner whether to perform every routine check.

## Fix or test

Act on a verified operational problem promptly. For a performance verdict use a comparable window and diagnosis; a documented planned challenger can be prepared separately. A tracked zero-order window can justify a fresh test without a fabricated CPA ceiling; attribution uncertainty still needs investigation.

## Recreate

Use a new campaign only when the strategy or required isolation materially changes. Record the failed hypothesis, chosen alternative and disposition of the old campaign. Duplicating to reset learning is not a reasoned strategy.

## Scale or stop

Scale requires verified business contribution, the strategy's repeatability design and authority. A proposed pause must name its evidence and exact object; prepare the saved approval route. Uncertainty alone does not authorize uncontrolled spending or an unapproved mutation.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Choose HOLD for immature/underexposed or satisfactory delivery, FIX for a verified repair, TEST for a learning question, REFRESH for diagnosed creative weakness, RECREATE for a materially different strategy, PAUSE for supported loss/operational conditions and SCALE for a verified growth case. Routine healthy checking does not require changing ads.

A planned challenger uses test_kind: planned-challenger and exposure_plan with question/comparison; it need not declare the control unsuccessful. ALLOCATE supplies exact existing ad-set target IDs/from/to daily amounts without increasing total; AUDIENCE supplies a explicitly scoped website-event audience recipe. Date-based strategy reconsideration needs an explicit reason and changed evidence. Set a next trigger/time and preserve the rejected alternatives.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.


## Resume and explicit user stops

RESUME restarts verified paused delivery within authority after Purchase source, destination, hierarchy, unresolved issues and fresh funding checks. Reuse the original AGI spending baseline. A budget pause is reviewed when the next monthly allocation begins; a performance pause keeps management scheduled for a supported next decision. If the user asks to stop management, submit PAUSE with stop_management: true and preserve that terminal instruction.
