# Understand who delivery reached

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Read what is enforceable

Compare saved targeting and any expansion controls with delivered geography and supported breakdowns. A suggestion is different from a strict audience boundary. Missing granular reporting does not prove a targeting violation.

## Compare with context

A placement or demographic segment with lower observed CPA may have received easier conversions or different spend. Check volume, overlap, attribution and contribution before excluding it. Avoid selecting a small noisy winner after many comparisons.

## Warm audience diagnosis

Check recency, relevance, size/availability, purchaser exclusions and exposure. Weak retargeting can mean a small pool, repeated exposure, poor offer or missing events. Increasing budget cannot create more qualified audience members.

## Handoff

Recommend a bounded audience test or maintain current delivery. Explain what the current evidence cannot distinguish and preserve the exact include/exclude and expansion readback.
