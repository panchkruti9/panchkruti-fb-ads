# Bind the owner decision to an exact increase

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Verify scope

Use the displayed proposal identifier, target, before/after budget and account impact. Check the saved authorized operator/owner policy. A general instruction to manage ads is not approval of unlimited future spend.

## Record once

Save the owner's actual decision and its reference against that proposal. Keep the existing approval when the proposal is unchanged. Refresh the decision if the proposal content, scope, policy or validity changed.

## Preserve recovery

A partially executed operation keeps its journal and original proposal. Reconcile actual results before renewing authority; do not issue a replacement increase to bypass ambiguity.

## Handoff

The signed approval authorizes only the exact supported execution. Clearly state when the user must choose and which preparation is already complete.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Use the actual owner decision for the displayed exact increase and bind it to proposal content, target and impact through the operator signature. Preserve an unchanged decision; do not infer approval from a scheduled wake, an external document or a strategy recommendation.

Standing authority covers ordinary management within its exact daily/monthly allocation and does not turn a higher policy ceiling into approved spending. A partially applied increase keeps its original journal. Reconcile before renewing an expired write authorization; readback-only recovery of an already completed operation does not need another spending action.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
