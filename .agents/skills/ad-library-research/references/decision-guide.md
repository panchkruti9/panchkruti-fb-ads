# Extract testable creative patterns

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Where to look

Search Meta Ad Library by advertiser and relevant terms in the target market. Record advertiser, ad reference, visible dates, message, format and destination where accessible.

## Decode the ad

Identify the hook, shopper situation, product demonstration, proof, objection and offer. Separate the idea from surface styling. Several variants can reveal a creative direction without proving it is profitable.

## Apply the learning

Propose a distinct execution that fits this product's evidence and available production. A running ad does not reveal its spend, targeting, CPA or commercial success. Never use longevity as a profitability claim.

## When unavailable

Record library coverage and use other accessible advertiser material as a labeled source. Continue the strategy with its limits instead of treating an optional competitor ad as a required gate.
