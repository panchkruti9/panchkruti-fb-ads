# Build a buyer hypothesis from evidence

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Where to look

Use product-level order aggregates, existing brand feedback, product questions, relevant reviews and discussions in the target market. Label external customers separately from this brand's customers.

## How to reason

Group statements by buying situation, desired progress, alternatives and objection. Prioritize repeated concrete behavior over broad demographic stereotypes. For each hypothesis write: situation, motivation, objection, credible response and the observation that would disprove it.

## Sparse history

Zero prior orders does not mean no viable audience. Infer a few plausible situations from the product and market, label them as hypotheses and choose a testable first message. Avoid asking the owner to supply research the agent can perform.

## Useful handoff

Connect each segment to a creative question and landing-page requirement. A persona is messaging guidance; Audience Planning separately verifies whether any targeting control is available.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Start with buying situations, not demographic stereotypes. Extract what shoppers were trying to achieve, what triggered a search, the alternative they considered and the objection that prevented purchase. Prefer concrete repeated statements; keep brand customer evidence separate from competitor reviews and public discussions. Record language suitable for a hypothesis without inventing quotations or testimonials.

With no retained orders, compare plausible situations from product use, pricing, questions and alternatives. Select one reason to buy and one important objection, then state what campaign behavior would contradict them. An inferred persona guides creative; it does not establish that Meta offers a corresponding targetable audience.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
