# Select useful, actually enforceable delivery

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Separate hypothesis and control

A shopper profile guides the message. It is not a claim that Meta exposes an interest or can identify a personal condition. Start with verified shipping countries and supported controls.

## Broad, warm or lookalike

Broad delivery can be a sensible first test with good offer/message fit. Warm delivery needs a verified relevant audience, recency and purchase objection. Lookalikes need a usable relevant seed and a budget that can answer the comparison. Explain why deferred alternatives add little information now.

## Availability

Read delivery status independently from operation warnings. Deliverable stale or populating audiences can be considered with limitations. Reject integrity restrictions and inactive/failed audiences. Use the exact include/exclude IDs and verify the saved targeting and expansion controls before launch.

## When a feature is unsupported

Do not invent a custom audience or silently claim a suggestion is a hard restriction. State the supported alternative, continue useful preparation and isolate any genuinely necessary capability work. Avoid product-condition names that imply sensitive health targeting.
