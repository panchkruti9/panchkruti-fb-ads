# Choose a bounded expansion

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Choose the mechanism

Consider more budget on the existing successful strategy before introducing new markets, audiences or duplicate campaigns. Each expansion changes comparability and should answer a distinct question.

## Size the change

Use verified margin, recent delivery, stock, remaining daily/monthly authority and the amount the next window can teach. A fixed 20 percent increment is not a universal Meta rule. Explain the selected before/after amount and downside.

## Model exposure

Project account-wide daily commitments and calendar-month spend under the proposed allocation. Keep actual spend, planned funding and Meta pacing separate. Include unmanaged active campaign commitments in headroom.

## Handoff

Save the exact target, current/proposed budget, supporting case, risks and next review. A growth scenario is not a guarantee that CPA stays constant as spend rises.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Consider the marginal next allocation, not only historic average ROAS. Verify stock and fulfillment capacity, current spending, recent contribution and how additional delivery may change acquisition cost or purchaser mix. Select a bounded increment from available headroom and uncertainty; 20 percent is not a universal platform rule.

Prefer useful redistribution inside existing authority before requesting more total spend. A different geography, bid/event, seed or offer is a new expansion hypothesis, not simply vertical scaling. Forecast a range of outcomes and the downside, then define a post-change review based on actual spend and purchase lag.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
