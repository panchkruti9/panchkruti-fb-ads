Read [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md#meta-ads) and the [brand rules](../../../docs/meta-ads/brand-rules.md) first. Use the saved campaign plan for settings and targets. Verify the account and currency before using account data. Honour the requested scope, including planning-only requests.

| Task | Read and do |
|---|---|
| Plan a new campaign or rethink an existing one | Use the [strategy playbook](../../../docs/meta-ads/strategy.md) to turn buyer evidence into an offer, funnel, creative brief, campaign structure, budget, and first test. Record the decisions and evidence in the [campaign plan](../../../docs/meta-ads/campaign-template.md). |
| Prepare creative or check launch readiness | Use the [Panchkruti creative workflow](../../../docs/meta-ads/creative.md), its brief template, and the [skincare and purchase-tracking checks](../../../docs/meta-ads/skincare.md). When the request includes build work, use the [runbook](../../../docs/meta-ads/runbook.md) for the operator handoff. |
| Diagnose, optimize, or scale | Use the [decision playbook](../../../docs/meta-ads/optimize.md) with the saved plan, comparable Meta and Shopify evidence, and recent changes. Record the decision, evidence, and next check. Use the runbook for any requested operator preview. |

Use only verified `agi-` account objects for campaign research and optimization. Use total account spend only for budget checks. When eligible history is sparse, use customer and market evidence and state what remains uncertain. Record campaign learnings with their evidence and scope so the next test builds on them.

Open the relevant row in the [local reference index](references/sources.md) when making a strategy, creative, tracking, testing, or review decision. The rules and decisions in this project govern how the source examples apply.
