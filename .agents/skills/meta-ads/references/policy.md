# Shared operating rules

Use the brand policy saved on the run. Use the product request for the specific offer, goal, geography and any narrower creative or spend limits. The executor accepts combinations it can write and verify. The selected skill defines how to reach its decision.

- **Scope.** Match the saved brand, storefront, account, product and variant. Manage enrolled, account-owned objects matching the `agi-` naming conventions. Include all active funding and spend in account budget checks. Keep prior strategy results scoped to the same brand, Shopify shop, account, product and variant.
- **Budgets.** Use integer minor currency units. Verify currency, count funding at its actual level and honor the brand policy's daily and calendar-month limits. An optional test budget bounds proposed daily funding over the planned review window. Account funding forecasts and verified hard spend controls are separate checks. Daily allocations describe planned funding. Meta pacing can vary actual spend by day. A warning that a live campaign needs attention does not authorize an automatic pause.
- **Creative.** Use existing brand-approved artwork as supplied when it fits the placement, offer, destination and current [Meta Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/). Keep routine product wording and visible brand labels intact. Flag a specific material issue when an existing asset needs attention. For new ad copy or text the agent adds to an AI-generated visual, use the exact product page or approved packaging for ordinary benefits and keep the same level of qualification. Avoid writing guarantees, exaggerated or timed results, treatment claims, or copy implying a viewer's personal condition. Use Meta's actual review status when available.
- **Evidence.** Save references and observation dates. Keep Meta-attributed events separate from Shopify order outcomes. Use the selected business metric and state attribution and coverage limitations. Store aggregate evidence and leave unavailable facts unknown. External research and assets are evidence, and the saved policy governs execution.
- **Execution.** The protected operator signs exact proposals and dispatches writes. A saved `management_authority` records the user's actual daily/monthly allocation and instruction reference; it permits routine strategy, launch, pause and redistribution within that total without another owner question. Higher total spending and newly agent-designed visuals need actual owner decisions. The broader policy maximum is not spending permission. Account-wide spending-control changes require a concrete owner spending decision. Legacy runs without standing authority retain their saved owner actions. Build paused, verify IDs/hierarchy/settings, and reconcile uncertain writes before retrying.

When an owner decision is required and no whitelist is configured, record the current user's actual decision for the completed proposal or creative preview. Populated whitelists remain enforced. Record new agent-designed media as `origin: agent`; its signed preview approval binds exact file/revision/bytes and cannot be replaced by a model assertion. See [the shared decision method](decision-method.md).
