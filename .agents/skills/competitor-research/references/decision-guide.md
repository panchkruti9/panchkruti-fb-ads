# Find a credible reason to choose this offer

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Where to look

Inspect alternatives a buyer in the assigned country can actually purchase, including substitutes outside the exact product category. Record current delivered price, contents, proof, terms and purchase friction.

## Compare fairly

Compare total value and the shopper job, not isolated list prices. Note whether discounts, shipping, pack sizes or subscriptions change the comparison. Record dates and visible facts; leave competitors' margins and sales unknown.

## Choose the opportunity

Find an advantage the assigned product can substantiate: clearer use, convenient contents, relevant proof, service or a competitive delivered offer. A competitor weakness is a hypothesis until shopper evidence supports its importance.

## Useful handoff

Give strategy a small ranked set of opportunities, the evidence and the constraint. Do not copy artwork, invent testimonials or claim superiority from an incomplete comparison.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Choose alternatives the same shopper can actually purchase in the assigned market, including doing nothing or buying components separately. Compare delivered price, quantity, use, shipping, returns, positioning and proof on equivalent terms. A large discount against a different pack size is not necessarily better value.

Separate an observable offer difference from an inferred positioning opportunity. If alternatives compete on trust, identify what this brand can honestly demonstrate rather than copying their claims. If pricing changes, refresh the affected comparison. Hand off a distinct buying reason and objection to test; do not report competitors as profitable without their verified business evidence.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
