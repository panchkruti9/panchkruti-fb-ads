# Measure delivery without confusing attribution and sales

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Choose the window

Use the account timezone, complete dates, recorded reporting lag and first full day after the last material edit. Compare equal compatible windows; exclude mixed launch/edit days.

## Diagnose the path

Read spend and delivery, outbound response, landing-page arrivals, checkout signals and attributed purchases where returned. Use consistent denominators: CPM is spend/impressions times 1000; link response and landing-page rate must identify their event definitions. Missing breakdown data stays unknown.

## Read safely

Scope Insights to the managed objects and needed fields. Split broad date ranges or heavy breakdowns on provider limits; preserve successful independent reads. Never sum daily reach to invent unique period reach or average percentages without their denominators.

## Handoff

Explain which stage changed, whether the window is comparable and what still needs Shopify confirmation. A high click rate alone does not establish profitable customer acquisition.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Read comparable campaign/ad-set/ad metrics with exact dates, account timezone, event definitions and attribution basis. Use spend, impressions, outbound response, arrivals, checkout and attributed purchases as a path. Calculate rates from summed numerators/denominators; never add daily reach to invent unique period reach. Split heavy Insights queries on provider limits and preserve independent successful results.

Use evidence_assessment to distinguish underexposure, lag, usable results and missing measurement. An ad receiving little spend is inconclusive. A Purchase learning label is not a purchase outcome. When a new challenger was added, inspect object_changes and actual exposure; avoid declaring the unchanged control newly launched or treating changing audience mix as randomized evidence.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
