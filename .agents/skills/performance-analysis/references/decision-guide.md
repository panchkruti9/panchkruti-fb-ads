# Measure delivery without confusing attribution and sales

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Choose the window

Use the account timezone, complete dates, recorded reporting lag and first full day after the last material edit. Compare equal compatible windows; exclude mixed launch/edit days.

## Diagnose the path

Read spend and delivery, outbound response, landing-page arrivals, checkout signals and attributed purchases where returned. Use consistent denominators: CPM is spend/impressions times 1000; link response and landing-page rate must identify their event definitions. Missing breakdown data stays unknown.

## Read safely

Scope Insights to the managed objects and needed fields. Split broad date ranges or heavy breakdowns on provider limits; preserve successful independent reads. Never sum daily reach to invent unique period reach or average percentages without their denominators.

## Handoff

Explain which stage changed, whether the window is comparable and what still needs Shopify confirmation. A high click rate alone does not establish profitable customer acquisition.
