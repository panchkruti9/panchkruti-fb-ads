# Product listing design

## Understand the product and reference

- Inspect the actual product, packaging, labels and product details. For bundles, identify each item, size, ingredients and benefits separately. Use verified sources; never invent ingredients, percentages, certifications, results or reviews.
- Inspect every image in the listing the user specifies as the content-flow reference. Record the purpose and order of each image. Inspect design inspiration separately for photography, composition, typography and color; never copy another brand's claims or packaging.
- Follow the requested content sequence. Each image must have a distinct job and address the customer's actual concern. Do not duplicate layouts, messages or imagery. Ingredient images explain ingredients and benefits; directions belong in usage images.

## Design and production

- Make imagery feel like a coherent, premium photographic campaign: natural lighting and textures, varied compositions and crops, realistic product photography, clear hierarchy, restrained typography and intentional whitespace. Avoid oversized headings, generic AI-looking scenes, repeated props and unnecessary decorative badges.
- Preserve actual packaging, labels, proportions and product identity exactly. Do not add a separate logo when the user says not to. Do not present regenerated packaging as exact if text, branding, seals or details changed. Use the original product assets; if the tools cannot preserve them faithfully, disclose the limitation and do not silently publish inaccurate images.
- Follow the user's requested aspect ratio and channel requirements. When the user requests a white-background main image, show only the included products on white with no added text or props. Keep separately sold products clearly distinguished from bundle contents.
- Respect the reference's content logic without copying its branding. Use a product-specific story and verified benefits; never transfer another product's trial results, photos of results or customer testimonials to the new product.

## Listing and verification

- Inspect comparable store listings for the main description, detailed description, benefits, ingredients, SEO and product metafields. Follow the user's preferred structure and the existing metafield names and types where applicable.
- Review every output for accurate product identity and text, content relevance, natural appearance, distinct composition, size, sequence and adherence to the user's reference. Treat AI-generated text, hands, packaging and claims as requiring careful inspection.
- Prepare and check replacements before removing an existing gallery. Change only the intended listing and assets, preserve requested draft status, and verify the saved image order and content. Report unfinished work honestly.
- Chat-only means no editing, uploads, publishing or deletion. Follow the user's instructions and existing authorizations; do not infer approval for unrelated actions.
