# Release the verified purchase experience

Inspect exact paused objects, placement previews, offer, destination, usable checkout, Purchase source and spending scope. Verify actual rendering, not only that preview markup exists. Store-wide event activity is useful but does not prove product-specific deduplication or a paid test order. Collect available evidence independently and retain limitations.

Use the current signed operator route. With standing management allocation and approved creative, launching is routine agent work; do not introduce another owner question. A higher total allocation or account-wide spending-control increase needs the exact completed proposal. New generated media needs finished-preview approval before build. Existing approvals remain valid for unchanged approved scope, with readback recovery for already completed writes.

Activate known children and parent in the verified order, reconcile timeouts and distinguish configured ACTIVE from effective delivery. Preserve campaign-scoped spending baselines; first observed delivery starts the exposure assessment and remains distinct from the verified launch timestamp. Prepaid funding uses native controls and no postpay-only cap update.

After release record the current allocation, first delivery, reporting lag, evidence question and next review. If delivery is still in review, report that state and the next operational check rather than claiming purchases or a live delivery result.
