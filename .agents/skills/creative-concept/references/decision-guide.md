# Create a distinct reason to pay attention

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Build the idea

Choose one shopper question, one benefit at the product's supported wording level and one visible way to demonstrate it. Define the opening, product reveal, proof or explanation, objection and action.

## Design a useful contrast

Different buying reasons or demonstrations teach more than changing a font. For example, a use demonstration and a verified value explanation test different objections. Keep offer and audience comparable when the question concerns the message.

## Production knowledge

Specify placement, aspect ratio, authentic pack/reference images, mandatory wording, text hierarchy, safe crop, and what must be visible on a phone. For video include sequence, spoken words, captions and poster frame. Existing approved artwork can be a valid starting asset.

## Before handoff

Check that production can actually make the concept with available assets. If the main promise cannot be shown honestly, choose another concept rather than asking copy to hide a visual contradiction.
