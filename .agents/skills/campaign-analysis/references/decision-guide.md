# Locate the main constraint

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Combine the evidence

Read strategy intent, delivery, Shopify business outcomes, creative and audience findings, stock, offer and account health. Check comparability and evidence gaps before ranking causes.

## Use a causal sequence

No delivery: inspect account, review, budget and audience. Delivery without arrivals: inspect message response, URL and measurement. Arrivals without checkout: inspect value and page friction. Checkout without payment: inspect payment, shipping and stock.

## Rank explanations

State the most plausible constraint and competing explanations. Prefer the smallest useful check that can distinguish them. A weak top-line ROAS number alone does not identify a creative or audience fault.

## Handoff

Give Optimization Decision a prioritized diagnosis, an available action, the likely business effect, evidence maturity and a specific next review.

Read the shared [operating knowledge](../../meta-ads/references/operating-knowledge.md) for source references and evidence limits. The current skill's output schema remains authoritative.

## Applied purchase decisions

Rank explanations through the purchase path: delivery, response, landing-page arrival, product intent, checkout, payment, retained order and contribution. Identify which stage changed and the strongest competing explanation. A low ROAS by itself does not identify a creative, audience or offer failure.

For a promising CTR with poor sales, check message/offer match, loaded destination, shipping and checkout before adding interests. For paid sales with negative contribution, examine price/cost/refunds/acquisition economics before increasing spend. Report one prioritized constraint, its evidence quality, the smallest useful intervention and what observation would disprove the diagnosis.

Read the [shared decision method](../../meta-ads/references/decision-method.md) for calculations, evidence grades, scenario diagnosis, authority and maintained source links. Preserve a dated observation versus hypothesis distinction in the handoff.
