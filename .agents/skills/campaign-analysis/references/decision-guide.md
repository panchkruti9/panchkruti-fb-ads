# Locate the main constraint

This is the system's operating method. Account settings and current provider capabilities still require readback.

## Combine the evidence

Read strategy intent, delivery, Shopify business outcomes, creative and audience findings, stock, offer and account health. Check comparability and evidence gaps before ranking causes.

## Use a causal sequence

No delivery: inspect account, review, budget and audience. Delivery without arrivals: inspect message response, URL and measurement. Arrivals without checkout: inspect value and page friction. Checkout without payment: inspect payment, shipping and stock.

## Rank explanations

State the most plausible constraint and competing explanations. Prefer the smallest useful check that can distinguish them. A weak top-line ROAS number alone does not identify a creative or audience fault.

## Handoff

Give Optimization Decision a prioritized diagnosis, an available action, the likely business effect, evidence maturity and a specific next review.
